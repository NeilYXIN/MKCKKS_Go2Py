# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who participates in this project must be able to do so free of
harassment, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Direct criticism at the work, not at the person who produced it.
- Accept review feedback gracefully, and give it constructively.
- Respect the confidentiality of vulnerability reports; see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Disclose AI assistance in contributions, as described in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, insults, and sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Publicly disclosing an unfixed vulnerability outside the coordinated process.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and commit history, and whenever someone is representing
the project in public.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at <96xinyang@gmail.com>. If the
report concerns the maintainer, or you would rather not use email, use GitHub's
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) form.

Reports are handled confidentially. You will receive an acknowledgement within
seven days. Please include what happened, where, when, and any links or
screenshots; you do not need to interpret or categorize the behaviour.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will investigate every report and respond in a way they judge
proportionate to the incident. Depending on severity and whether the behaviour
is repeated, a response may be a private warning, a request for a public
apology, removal or editing of the content in question, a temporary restriction
on participation, or a permanent ban. Maintainers will explain the reason for
any enforcement action to the person concerned.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may themselves
face action from the other maintainers, as described in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
